Astrology Consultation

Dr. Umang Nath Sharma - carrying forward 400+ years of Kashi's Vedic tradition - offers personal astrology consultations. Kundli analysis, planetary dosh remedies, marriage matching, and solutions for life's challenges. Visit us in person between 9 AM - 12 PM, or book an appointment online.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Vipreet Pratyangira Paath

An extremely powerful tantric ritual dedicated to Goddess Pratyangira. 'Vipreet' means reverse - returning black magic and negative energy back to the sender. Performed by 7 learned Brahmins.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Dus Mahavidya Paath

A sacred tantric ritual dedicated to the ten Mahavidyas (Kali, Tara, Tripura Sundari, etc.) performed by 5 Brahmins for spiritual power and protection.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Tripindi Shradh (Pitru Pooja)

Performed at Pishach Mochan in Kashi for the pacification of Pitru Dosh and finding peace for unsatisfied ancestral souls. Dedicated to Brahma, Vishnu, and Mahesh.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Kalsarp Dosh Nivaran

Performed to reduce the negative effects of Rahu and Ketu planets. This pooja is conducted on Panchami Tithi at the famous Nag Koop Temple in Kashi.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Rudrabhishek

A highly sacred and powerful abhishekam of Lord Shiva using water, milk, curd, ghee, honey, etc. on the Shivling.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Kanakdhara Stotra Paath

A powerful hymn composed by Adi Shankaracharya dedicated to Goddess Lakshmi. 'Kanakdhara' means a shower of gold. It opens new paths for stalled businesses.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Ganesh Atharvashirsha

A special pooja and recitation of the Ganesh Atharvashirsha dedicated to Lord Ganesha for intellect, success, and removing obstacles.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Shree Suktam Paath

A sacred hymn from the Rigveda dedicated to Goddess Lakshmi, performed for wealth, prosperity, and financial stability.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Purush Suktam Paath

An important Vedic hymn from the Rigveda dedicated to Lord Vishnu/Narayana, excellent for health, long life, and spiritual upliftment.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Kumbh Vivah (Manglik Dosh)

A symbolic marriage performed before the actual wedding to permanently eliminate the negative effects of Manglik Dosh in the horoscope.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Griha Pravesh & Vastu Shanti Pujan

Griha Pravesh Pujan is an essential ritual performed before moving into a new residence - the Vastu Devata and the presiding deities of the directions are worshipped to free the home of defects and make it auspicious.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Navgrah Shanti & Rudrabhishek

The Navgrah Shanti ritual pacifies the afflictions of all nine planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu) and, combined with Rudrabhishek, removes planetary obstacles from life.

→ Book Directly on WhatsApp Mahamrityunjaya Jaap Anushthan

Mahamrityunjaya Jaap is a supremely powerful mantra practice dedicated to Lord Shiva, performed especially to counter serious illness, obstacles to longevity, and the fear of untimely death.
